About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Operations at the Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai, specializing in Operations Management, Supply Chain Management and Resilience, and Stochastic Simulation Modelling. Driven by a passion to bridge rigorous theory and business practice, my research focuses on developing evidence-based models for complex systems in healthcare, manufacturing, and public policy. I have authored various peer-reviewed articles in top-tier journals (such as Production Planning and Control, Supply Chain Management, Nature's Scientific Reports, and BMJ Open), published the textbook 'Design Thinking with Problem-Solving Techniques' with PHI Learning, and managed key research projects in collaboration with UNICEF and the Government of Tamil Nadu.

Objective

To mentor, inspire and empower students beyond academics to facilitate them to meaningfully contribute to humanity. I am equally committed to research to bridge theory and practice, enable informed policymaking and evidence-based business decisions.

Research Interests

  • Operations Management
  • Supply Chain Management and Resilience
  • Modeling and Simulation of real-time systems
  • Interdisciplinary research: Healthcare, Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering
  • Sustainability

Interactive Web Materials

Welcome to the Operations & Policy Analytics Portal. Below are the custom-engineered interactive web platforms, scientific calculators, and simulation dashboards developed by Dr. Narassima M.S. These state-of-the-art analytical tools were designed to bridge the gap between theoretical optimization models and real-world implementation, offering robust solutions for business workflow planning, public health epidemiological analysis, and macroeconomic policy formulation.

OM PGPM Planning Dashboard

A sophisticated operations simulation and capacity modeling dashboard. It facilitates dynamic workflow mapping, scheduling coordination, bottleneck detection, and queueing-theory allocations for corporate supply chain processes. Designed as an active learning module for strategic operations analysis.

Explore Dashboard

COVID-19 DALY Calculator

An interactive, scientifically validated web-based calculator developed to evaluate and quantify public health impacts. It computes Disability-Adjusted Life Years (DALY) based on Years of Life Lost (YLL) and Years Lived with Disability (YLD) to guide evidence-based epidemic policy decisions.

Non-Health GDP Loss Estimator

An advanced macroeconomic econometric simulator. This tool allows researchers and economists to evaluate, simulate, and project sectoral non-health Gross Domestic Product (GDP) losses resulting from workforce morbidity, mortality, and associated macroeconomic pandemic shockwaves.
